    How to Pick a Podcast Name That Actually Sticks

    Most listeners discover new shows through word of mouth or a quick search inside Spotify and Apple Podcasts. Use these four producer-tested rules before you design your cover art.

    01

    Pass the "Bar Test"

    Say your title out loud to a friend across a noisy room. If they have to ask how to spell it—or if it uses clever misspellings like "Kast" or "Lyfe"—they will never find your show later when they open their phone.

    02

    Respect the 30-Character Cutoff

    Apple Podcasts and Spotify truncate long show names on mobile browse screens and lock-screen players. Keep your core title under 30 characters so the best part of the name stays visible everywhere.

    03

    Skip Generic Filler Words

    Words like "Ultimate," "Everything," "Chronicles," or tacking "The ___ Podcast" onto a broad topic make your show blend into hundreds of inactive feeds. Pair one clear subject word with a specific angle, mood, or point of view.

    04

    Leave Yourself Room to Grow

    Naming a show "2026 Side Hustles for College Juniors" feels focused on day one, then boxes you in by episode twenty. Pick a title tied to the ongoing curiosity behind your show, not a single trend or year.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Straight answers to the naming questions independent hosts ask most before submitting their RSS feed.

    How long should a podcast name be?

    Aim for 2 to 4 words, or under 30 characters. Apple Podcasts and Spotify start cutting off longer titles on phone screens, especially inside browse carousels and lock-screen players. Short titles are also much easier to read on small cover art.

    Should I put the word "Podcast" in my show title?

    Usually, no. Listeners are already inside a podcast app when they find your show, so adding "Podcast" at the end wastes character space. The only exception is if your name sounds confusing without it or if the word "Show" or "Podcast" is part of the rhythm you want.

    How do I check if a podcast name is already taken?

    Search the exact title on both Apple Podcasts and Spotify first—those two directories catch most active and dormant shows. Next, check Google, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and the USPTO trademark database (TESS) if you plan to monetize or sell merch.

    Can I change my podcast name later on Apple Podcasts and Spotify?

    Yes. Updating the title in your podcast host updates your RSS feed, and Apple Podcasts and Spotify typically reflect the change within 24 to 48 hours. Still, rebranding after you have listeners can hurt word-of-mouth referrals, so it pays to settle on a solid title before episode one.

    Should my podcast name include keywords for search?

    If you run an educational, business, or niche how-to show, putting one clear topic word in the title or subtitle helps people find you in Apple and Spotify search. Just keep it natural—Apple actively rejects shows that stuff a string of comma-separated keywords into the title field.
